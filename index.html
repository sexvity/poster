YTK SHORTS

Free desktop app for Windows

YTK SHORTS helps creators publish short vertical videos to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts from one place. Pick a video, review the AI-suggested title, description and hashtags, choose a cover frame, and post to the accounts you connect.

Download for Windows

What it does

  • Signs in to your TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit. You choose exactly what to authorize.
  • Shows your TikTok profile, the privacy options and interaction settings TikTok allows for your account, and posts only when you click Post.
  • Suggests an English title, description and hashtags by analysing frames of your video with AI, and lets you edit everything before posting.
  • Picks a cover frame and compresses the video to fit each platform's size limits.
  • Works with several accounts and lets you choose where each video goes.

Free and private

YTK SHORTS is free for everyone. Your videos and sign-in tokens stay on your computer. See the Privacy Policy for details.
