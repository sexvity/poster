Postador: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Postador is a personal desktop tool. Its owner uses it to upload their own videos to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

What we collect

Nothing. Postador does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There is no website account, no analytics and no advertising.

Where your information is kept

Login tokens issued by TikTok, Google and Meta are kept only in a file on the owner's own computer. Videos are sent directly from that computer to the platforms the owner chooses.

Third parties

When the owner posts, the video and its caption go to TikTok, YouTube (Google) or Instagram (Meta). Those services handle the data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Delete the Postador folder on your computer, and revoke access in your TikTok, Google or Meta account settings.
