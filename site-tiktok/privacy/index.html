Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how YTK SHORTS ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is a free Windows desktop program that runs on your computer.

Information the app uses

  • TikTok account data: when you connect TikTok, the app receives an access token and refresh token, and reads your public profile name and avatar (scope user.info.basic) and your posting options. With the video.publish scope it uploads the videos you choose to post.
  • Your videos and text: the files you select, plus the title, description and hashtags you write or approve.
  • Other platforms: if you connect YouTube or Instagram, the app stores the access tokens those platforms provide, only to post on your behalf.

How it is used

Only to show your connected account and to publish the content you choose, when you click Post. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or post anything without your action.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are saved in a local file on your computer. We do not run a database of users. Videos are uploaded directly from your computer to the platform you select.

Third-party services

  • Sign-in relay: a small server (Cloudflare Workers) exchanges TikTok authorization codes for tokens so that app secrets are not shipped inside the program. It does not store your tokens or content.
  • AI text suggestions: to suggest titles and hashtags, a few frames of your video and your topic note are sent to Google's Gemini API using your own API key.
  • Platforms: TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google) process the data you send to them under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can disconnect TikTok at any time in the app (this revokes access and deletes the local tokens), or remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's local settings file removes all stored data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
